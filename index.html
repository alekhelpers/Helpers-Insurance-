FAQ

Straight answers before you book

Do you charge a fee for consulting?

No. Like most independent agents, compensation comes from the carrier when you enroll. You don’t get an invoice for the guidance.

What happens on the free 15-minute call?

You tell Alek your situation: state, household, budget, doctors, timeline. He outlines next steps. No pressure to buy on the call.

Can you help small businesses with only 2–3 people?

Yes. Micro-groups and owner-only setups are common. Alek walks through group versus individual and what usually fits the budget. There is no minimum employee count and no minimum contribution requirement on the small-business plans he shops.

Why use an agent instead of going straight to a carrier?

One carrier only shows their own products. An independent agent compares options for your state.

Do I have to wait for open enrollment?

No. Private insurance can be purchased any time of year. Marketplace plans still have open-enrollment windows, or special enrollment after a life event. Small-business plans Alek shops are year-round. He will map what fits.
