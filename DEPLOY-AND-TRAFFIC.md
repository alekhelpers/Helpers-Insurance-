# Put Helpers Insurance online and get found

The live domain **https://www.helpershealthinsurance.com** currently shows a paused ClickFunnels page. Search engines and ads cannot send people to a paused funnel. Point that domain at this folder, then do the local listings below.

## 1. Publish this site (do this first)

Fastest path: [Netlify Drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop)

1. Open Netlify Drop and upload the whole `helpers-insurance-site` folder.
2. Confirm the home page loads, then the booking block, then a guide such as `/the-woodlands/`.
3. In Netlify: **Domain management → Add custom domain** → `helpershealthinsurance.com` and `www.helpershealthinsurance.com`.
4. At your domain registrar (wherever the name is parked), change DNS from ClickFunnels to the Netlify nameservers or the A/CNAME records Netlify shows.
5. After HTTPS is green, submit `https://www.helpershealthinsurance.com/sitemap.xml` in [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console) and [Bing Webmaster Tools](https://www.bing.com/webmasters).

The first form submission to `alek.helpers@gmail.com` will send a **FormSubmit confirmation email**. Click it once or messages will not arrive.

Old ClickFunnels and Wix URLs already redirect:

- `/schedule-a-call` → booking section
- old `/blog/...` posts → the new guides
