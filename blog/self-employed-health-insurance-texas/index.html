Self-employed health insurance in Texas

If you do not have an employer plan, you still have a path. The right door depends on income, health history, and which Houston-area doctors you need.

Most self-employed people around The Woodlands start on Healthcare.gov because the ads are loud. That is a valid door. It is not the only door. Alek Timmons compares marketplace plans and private PPO options for Texas 1099 households.

Marketplace (ACA) plans

You cannot be turned down for a pre-existing condition. Premium tax credits may lower the monthly cost if household income lands in the credit range. Networks are often narrower than a nationwide PPO. Open enrollment still applies unless you have a qualifying life event.

Private PPO plans

These can offer broader access and year-round start dates, and they often require medical underwriting. If you have recent major conditions, this door may not open. Alek will say so instead of wasting a week.

What to have ready

  • Zip code and household size.
  • A realistic income range for the year.
  • Doctors, specialists, and a medication list.
  • Any recent job loss, marriage, or move.
