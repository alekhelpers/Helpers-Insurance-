PPO vs HMO in Texas

The logo on the card matters less than whether your specialist is in-network and whether you need a referral to get there.

Book my free 15-min call Call 713-304-6649

HMO and EPO plans in Texas usually keep you inside a defined network. Referrals are common on HMOs. PPO plans typically allow out-of-network care at a higher cost and make specialist visits easier. That flexibility is why The Woodlands families ask for a PPO when they use Houston Methodist, travel, or want a second opinion without a gatekeeper.

When an HMO is enough

If every doctor you use is already in a tight local network and you want the lowest premium, an HMO or EPO can be the right tool. Alek will say so.

When a PPO is the point

Kids with specialists in different systems, snowbirds, or anyone who already got a surprise bill from a narrow network plan. Private PPOs can require underwriting; marketplace PPOs (when available) cannot reject you for health history.
